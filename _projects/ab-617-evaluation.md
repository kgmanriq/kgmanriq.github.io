Co-authored a state-commissioned evaluation of California's Community Air Monitoring Program, analyzing 70+ interviews across 10 pilot communities to assess whether local resident input was successfully incorporated into policy design and execution.

The report was widely cited by national think tanks, including the Brookings Institution and the Center for American Progress.
