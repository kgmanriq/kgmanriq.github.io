About Me

I’m Katrina, a Senior Strategy Consultant in KPMG’s Deal Advisory and Strategy practice. For nearly a decade I’ve helped government and heavy-infrastructure clients adopt complex technology and innovation responsibly. I’m now bringing that lens to AI safety.

I believe large language models are not ideologically neutral: their behavior is shaped by the data they’re trained on, the incentives they’re built to meet, the values they’re configured to hold, and the context they’re deployed in. I believe safer AI cannot be evaluated in isolation from the broader ecosystem they are apart of. I’m interested in studying what AI is, rather than what we expect it to become, using systems and first-principles thinking to ask better questions about how models behave and how to make them safer.

I hold a Master of Public Policy from the University of Southern California and a Bachelor of Arts in History and English from the University of California, Davis. I’m a former David Bohnett Leadership Fellow and have interned with the California Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation, the San Francisco Board of Supervisors, and the Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors.
